# Footage to record

Record at 1080p, 30 fps, with the phone on a stand or tripod that doesn't move (except in recording 7). Keep all four board corners in frame with a little margin. Save the PGN of each game, either by writing the moves down or by entering them on Lichess afterward.

Drop the files in this folder with these names. The video and its PGN should share a name: `01-overhead-daylight.mp4` and `01-overhead-daylight.pgn`.

| # | Name | Setup | What to play |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01-overhead-daylight | Phone straight overhead, daylight, vinyl tournament board, plastic Staunton pieces | A full game, about 40 moves, normal pace |
| 2 | 02-side-45 | Same board and pieces, camera at about 45 degrees from one side | The same game as recording 1 |
| 3 | 03-lamp-evening | Overhead, evening, one lamp, strong shadows | Any full game |
| 4 | 04-blitz | Overhead, good light | Blitz with fast moves. A few times, let both players move before anyone pulls their hand back |
| 5 | 05-torture | Overhead, good light | `torture-game.pgn` in this folder. Follow the notes in the braces |
| 5b | 05b-checkmate | Same setup | `checkmate-game.pgn` (Legal's mate, 7 moves, ends in mate) |
| 6 | 06-hands-no-moves | Overhead, from the starting position | Two minutes with no moves: hover hands, straighten pieces, rest an arm on the edge of the frame |
| 7 | 07-camera-bump | Overhead | Any game. Around move 15, bump the stand so the view shifts a little, then keep playing |
| 8 | 08-wooden-dark | Wooden board, dark pieces, normal room light | Any full game |

Notes for the torture game:

- You need a spare white queen and a spare black knight. Both come off the board early (moves 10 and 11), so set them aside.
- The notes ask for pauses at a few moves (en passant, castling, a capture) so we see the in-between positions the app has to wait through.
- From move 32 the moves are scripted to set up the promotions. White resigns after 42...Nxc3+.

Also tell me the phone model, the iOS or Android version, and whether the phone was plugged in.
