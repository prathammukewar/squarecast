Squarecast

Put a phone on a stand over a chessboard. It records the game as you play.

Does any video leave my phone?

No. The camera picture is read on the phone and thrown away. Only the moves are kept, and they stay on the phone unless you share them.

Which phones work?

A recent iPhone or Android phone with Safari or Chrome. Plug it in for long games. A stand that holds the phone above the board, or above White's side looking down, works best.

Can I use it at a rated tournament?

The phone is organizer equipment: set it up with the tournament director's approval, and players never touch it. Rules about electronic devices are written for players, but check with your director before using it at a rated event.

How does it know the moves?

It never tries to recognize the pieces. It knows the starting position, so after each move it only has to work out which legal move explains what changed on the board. When two moves look alike it asks you.

Frame the board

Put the phone above the board, or above White's side looking down. Keep all four corners in view with a little space around them.

Mark the corners

Tap the four outer corners of the squares, not the border. Drag a corner to adjust it.

White's side

The bar marks the side White sits on.

Starting position

Set up the pieces and keep hands off the board for a moment.

Game details

All optional. They go into the PGN.
